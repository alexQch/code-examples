Interaction rules

        
    
          
  • Leaves are the truth. Only country codes are stored; region & Global state are derived. No contradictory parents.
    • 
          
  • Cascade both ways. Ticking East ticks its countries; ticking the last country auto-turns East to full; unticking one turns it mixed.
    • 
          
  • "Global except X" is preserved: unchecking a country under Global materialises the list minus that country.
    • 
          
  • Empty ≠ all. No selection shows an amber warning instead of silently meaning "everything".
    • 
          
  • Search matches aliases (UK→GB, APAC→East, USA→US) and keeps ancestors visible for context.
